FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does the AI make the underwriting decision?

No. Recommendations come from deterministic guideline rules. The LLM extracts data and explains the result, and a human underwriter always makes the final decision.

Which AI models does it use?

Google AI by default. It reads scans and messy documents, writes the summary and drafts the email. Without a key it runs on the rules engine only. If a model call fails or is rate-limited, that step retries and then falls back to offline logic.

Can it read scanned documents and spreadsheets?

Yes. Scans and images are read with AI OCR (needs your key). Text PDFs, CSV and Excel schedules of values and loss runs are parsed directly. Every extracted value shows its source page and a confidence indicator, and reviewers can correct it.

Is it connected to Guidewire or Duck Creek?

Not yet. The prototype is standalone. Integration through an API layer is on the roadmap.

Is the data real?

No. The demo uses synthetic submissions. The guidelines, risk model and valuation benchmarks are illustrative defaults, not a real carrier’s. Flood data comes from FEMA when the lookup is available.

How is the time saving measured?

The dashboard shows an estimate based on an assumed manual triage time. It is an assumption to be validated in a pilot, not a measured result.
